# Clinical safety
- CareSync does not diagnose, prescribe or change treatment. AI output is informational and requires clinician review.
- Alerts never replace emergency services. Every urgent alert tells users to contact local emergency services.
- Patient-specific alert thresholds can only be set by verified clinicians; defaults are a conservative safety net.
- Clinician-verified medicines cannot be altered by patients or caregivers.
- Signed clinical notes cannot be edited; corrections are recorded as amendments.
